About this policy

This Policy transparently explains how Table Tennis Manager processes personal data on its website, web application and future mobile apps. Brazil's LGPD and other mandatory rules apply according to the user's location.

2. Data we process

  • Account: username, email, role, protected password and verification records.
  • Sports management: player profiles, date of birth, playing hand, rankings, matches, results, competitions and statistics.
  • Organization: calendar, commitments, notes and financial information entered by the user.
  • Technical data: IP address, session, browser, access, failure and security logs.
  • Support: messages and data provided for assistance or rights requests.

We do not request banking passwords or full card details. Avoid entering unnecessary sensitive data in free-text fields.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to create and secure accounts, deliver contracted tools, synchronize sports information, provide support, prevent fraud, maintain technical records and comply with legal duties. Depending on the context, the legal basis may be contract performance, legal obligation, assessed legitimate interest or consent when specifically requested.

4. Sharing and transfers

We may use hosting, database, storage, email, monitoring and payment providers. They receive only the data needed for their service and must observe security and confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose data when legally required or to protect rights. We do not sell personal data.

Some providers may process data outside Brazil. We will apply mechanisms compatible with applicable law and identify key providers once the production environment is defined.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain data while an account is active and as needed to meet legal duties, resolve disputes, prevent fraud and preserve security records. Deletion requests are assessed against lawful retention requirements. Backups are removed through secure cycles.

Data subjects may request deletion of their account and associated data even while signed out.

6. Security

We apply access controls, encryption in transit, hashed passwords, user isolation, event logs, backups and security updates. No system is infallible; relevant incidents will be handled and communicated as required by law.

7. Your rights

You may request confirmation, access, correction, sharing information, portability where applicable, review of automated decisions, objection, anonymization, blocking or deletion where provided by law, and withdrawal of specific consent. Requests may require identity verification and are free of charge.

8. Minors

Accounts must be created by legally capable persons or authorized guardians. Adults, coaches and clubs recording information about minors must have appropriate authorization and limit data to what is needed for sports management.

Essential cookies
